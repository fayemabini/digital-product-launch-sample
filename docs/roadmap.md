# Roadmap & Milestones

## Phase 1 — Planning
**Week 1**

- Confirm launch goal
- Finalize scope
- Confirm owners
- Create milestone plan
- Identify dependencies
- Open risk log

**Exit criteria:** Scope, owners, target date, and success criteria are approved.

## Phase 2 — Content & Build
**Weeks 2–3**

- Finalize curriculum
- Draft landing page copy
- Build WooCommerce product
- Configure LearnDash course
- Produce lesson videos
- Draft email campaign
- Prepare learner onboarding

**Exit criteria:** Core deliverables are at least in review.

## Phase 3 — QA & Launch Prep
**Week 4**

- Test purchase flow
- Test course access
- Review video playback
- Review mobile experience
- Proofread launch copy
- Confirm email automation
- Confirm learner support process
- Resolve critical defects

**Exit criteria:** No blocker-level issues remain.

## Phase 4 — Launch
**Week 5**

- Publish landing page
- Activate checkout
- Send launch emails
- Monitor support issues
- Track early performance

## Phase 5 — Review
**Week 6**

- Review launch metrics
- Gather team feedback
- Document lessons learned
- Prioritize process improvements
