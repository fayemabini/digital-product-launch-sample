## Risk management approach

I do not only report a risk. I try to attach a recommendation.

For example:

> **Risk:** Video production is two days behind and could affect launch readiness.  
> **Recommendation:** Move non-critical promotional edits to post-launch, prioritize course videos first, and add a daily review checkpoint until the backlog is cleared.

This keeps escalation actionable rather than purely informational.
