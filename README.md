# Digital Product Launch — Sample Project

> Fictional portfolio project created to demonstrate how I structure and manage a digital product launch.

## Project overview

This sample project shows how I would manage the launch of a paid online learning product from planning through release.

The goal is to demonstrate how I work as a Project Manager: defining scope, breaking work into clear deliverables, assigning ownership, tracking dependencies, surfacing risks early, running QA, and keeping stakeholders informed.

## Scenario

A small digital education company is preparing to launch a new 4-week live online program.

The launch includes:

- Course curriculum and lesson materials
- Landing page and checkout setup
- Email campaign
- Video assets
- Learner onboarding
- Affiliate / partner support
- QA and launch readiness
- Post-launch review

## Success criteria

- Launch by the agreed target date
- All critical launch assets approved before release
- No blocker-level defects at launch
- Learner onboarding completed before the first live session
- Stakeholders receive clear weekly status updates
- Risks and decisions are escalated early, with recommendations

## How I would manage it

1. Clarify the business goal and launch date
2. Define scope, milestones, owners, and acceptance criteria
3. Break the project into workstreams
4. Track dependencies and blockers
5. Run weekly status reviews
6. Escalate risks with a recommendation attached
7. Complete launch-readiness QA
8. Run a retrospective after launch
